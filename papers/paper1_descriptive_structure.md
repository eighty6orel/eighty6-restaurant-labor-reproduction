# Paper 1 — Descriptive structure of the U.S. restaurant labor market

Reproduced via the eighty6 QCEW API; see `scripts/04_reproduce.py`. Covers **RQ1, RQ2, RQ5**. Inference is descriptive except RQ2 (within-county association in changes).

## Question

How large is the restaurant sector in the private wage bill and in employment, how much of the cross-section is between versus within counties, and did full-service and limited-service employment diverge in 2020–22?

## Data

`GET /v1/qcew/employment` with `ownership_code=5`, `size_code=0`. County aggregation levels 71 / 76 / 78; national 11 / 16 / 18. Industries: all-industry `10`, restaurants `7225` (2011+; `7221`+`7222` for 2001–2010), FSR `722511`/`722110`, LSR/other `722513`–`515` / `722211`–`213`. Disclosure code `N` and null wages or employment are dropped and never zero-filled.

## Constructs

| Construct | Formula |
|-----------|---------|
| Wage-bill share | `wage_7225 / wage_all` |
| Employment share | `emp_7225 / emp_all` |
| Employees per establishment | `emp_7225 / est_7225` |

## Headline findings (canonical RQ values)

- **RQ1.** U.S. private 7225 wage-bill share rose from **2.4534%** (2014Q1) to **2.7005%** (2024Q1). Employment share was **8.2808%** then **8.2466%** — about three times the wage-bill share (a pay penalty, not a “health” score).
- **RQ1.** In 2024Q1, **2,538** usable counties; mean wage-bill share **3.4473%**; P10 / P50 / P90 = **1.3743% / 3.1363% / 5.7926%**.
- **RQ1.** Of 2014–2024 county-quarter wage-share variance, **90.779%** is between counties and **9.221%** is within. The geography of mix is mostly a cross-section.
- **RQ2.** Within-county 4-quarter first differences: Δepe vs ΔAWW Pearson **−0.022** (7225), **+0.051** (FSR), **−0.129** (LSR). Association in changes, not a firm-power effect. Signs differ by format.
- **RQ5.** FSR employment fell **48.6%** from 2019Q4 (5,432,197) to 2020Q2 (2,791,554); LSR/other fell **17.2%** (5,358,195 → 4,434,843). By 2024Q4 FSR was still **2.5%** below 2019Q4; LSR/other was **4.7%** above.

## Caveats

County is not city. NAICS 7225 excludes catering and drinking places (7223/7224). Complete-case counties over-represent larger markets. The 2020–22 window is displayed, not residualized.
